eMudhra intermediate CA private keys compromised, forcing trust-store updates

First reported 2026-10-20

Simon Willison reports that the private keys of eight eMudhra intermediate certificate authority certificates have been compromised. These intermediates chain to roots trusted by the Mozilla, Microsoft, Apple, and Chrome root programs and many downstream trust stores. Python's uv 0.13.4, pip 26.2.2, and certifi 2026.10.19 have shipped updated certificate lists, and Linux distributions are being told to update their ca-certificates packages.

Why it mattersA compromised intermediate CA can let an attacker impersonate sites to any software that trusts its chain, so the fix is an urgent, system-wide certificate update rather than an AI-specific problem.
For buildersUpdate your CA bundles now (ca-certificates, certifi, pip, uv) and check that any AI SDKs or containers you ship pull in the refreshed trust lists.

Google open-sources TorchTPU, a native PyTorch backend for Cloud TPUs

First reported 2026-10-20

Google open-sourced TorchTPU, announced at the PyTorch Conference, as a native PyTorch device backend for Cloud TPUs co-developed with Meta's PyTorch team. It replaces PyTorch/XLA, which Google now recommends for new workloads, and includes vLLM, SGLang, and TorchTitan integrations. TechCrunch notes that Google follows Amazon in releasing tooling that makes it easier to move PyTorch apps off Nvidia GPUs.

Why it mattersA first-class PyTorch TPU backend lowers the switching cost away from Nvidia, which could shift where AI workloads run and how competitive accelerator pricing becomes.
For buildersIf you run PyTorch training or inference, you can now target TPUs with torch.device("tpu") rather than a separate XLA path, so it's worth benchmarking on TPU for cost.

Pros

  • Reduces lock-in to one GPU vendor for PyTorch teams
  • Open-source, Apache 2.0, with integrations into popular serving stacks

Cons

  • Early-version software (v0.2.0) may lack the maturity of CUDA-based paths
  • Performance and parity claims are vendor-reported so far
