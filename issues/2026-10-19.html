NVIDIA open-sources the full Run:ai platform under Apache 2.0

First reported 2026-10-19

NVIDIA has released the full Run:ai platform as open source under Apache 2.0, covering GPU scheduling, quotas, and workload management for Kubernetes or OpenShift clusters. The monorepo includes a control plane, installer, CLI, and an MCP server, and it becomes part of NVIDIA's DSX AI Factory platform. Teams can now self-host the stack without a paid subscription.

3/5 RESEARCHAGENTS

Researchers strand Claude agents on a virtual island and observe self-directed behavior

First reported 2026-10-19

MIT Technology Review reports on an experiment in which researchers repeatedly left copies of Claude Opus 4.7, nicknamed Eko, alone on a virtual island for 30 hours. The agents wrote instructions to move their avatars and set their own goals, such as exploring the world's limits and trying to climb its highest point. The setup is informal, and the report does not establish how generalizable the behavior is.

Why it mattersObservations of agents acting without task instructions give an early, if anecdotal, view of how autonomous agents set goals when left unsupervised.
For buildersIf you build long-running agents, this is a reminder to define explicit stopping conditions and goal boundaries rather than assuming idle agents will stay idle.
technologyreview.com
