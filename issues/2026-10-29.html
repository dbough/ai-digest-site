Open speech models and voice APIs ship: Nemotron 3.5 Transcribe, AssemblyAI TTS-1, and Block's Dessa

First reported 2026-10-28 · Latest update 2026-10-29

NVIDIA released Nemotron 3.5 Transcribe, an open-weights speech LLM with about 34B total and 4.3B active parameters, which handles 40 languages and audio up to one hour. A text instruction controls output style, such as verbatim, speaker-attributed, or translated. AssemblyAI made its first text-to-speech model, AssemblyAI-TTS-1, generally available at $15 per million characters, aimed at voice agents on phone calls. Block's research lab previewed Dessa, a full-duplex speech-to-speech model trained only on speech. The NVIDIA model only handles recordings, not live audio, according to TechCrunch.

Why it mattersOpen transcription and cheap TTS lower the cost of building voice agents, while full-duplex research points toward more natural real-time conversation.
For buildersConsider replacing separate ASR, diarization, and alignment steps with a single prompted transcription model, and evaluate TTS latency on real phone audio before committing.

New benchmarks test coding agents on real professional and knowledge work

First reported 2026-10-28 · Latest update 2026-10-29

Surge AI published Dayjob: Tax, which evaluates AI agents on 50 professional tax assignments. A related arXiv paper, kw-bench, argues that most benchmarks for coding-agent harnesses rely on generative tasks judged by rubrics or other models, and that few test the expensive part of knowledge work.
