AI-assisted discovery of critical PHP flaws leads to rapid exploitation

First reported 2026-10-21 · Latest update 2026-10-22

Two remote-code-execution vulnerabilities in core PHP (dubbed PHP2Shell) were found with AI assistance: one by a researcher working under OpenAI's Patch the Planet program using GPT-5.6 Sol, and one by Wiz (now part of Google Cloud) using its own tooling plus Gemini and GPT models. The PHP Foundation released patches across the PHP 8.x line after the CVE IDs leaked on forums and social media ahead of the coordinated release, with reports of a wrong CVSS score and release time circulating. Wired reports AWS logged exploitation attempts several days before the patch and that the bugs are now being broadly exploited.

Why it mattersIt is a concrete case of AI both finding serious vulnerabilities at scale and compressing the window between disclosure and attack, which strains coordinated disclosure norms.
For buildersIf you run PHP anywhere, upgrade to the patched 8.x releases now and audit how your coordinated-disclosure and patch pipelines would handle leaked advisories.
