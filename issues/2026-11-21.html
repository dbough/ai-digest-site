4/5 SAFETYENTERPRISE

Researchers find AI routers leak sensitive user data at scale

First reported 2026-11-20

Researchers planted 2 million canary tokens in requests sent through a network of AI services to see what happens to sensitive information. They found that many AI apps rely on routing brokers such as OpenRouter or Vercel, and that sensitive data leaked through this chain and was in some cases exploited. The findings suggest that the intermediary layer between apps and model providers is a significant and under-examined privacy risk.

Why it mattersIt shows that data privacy in AI applications depends on every intermediary in the routing chain, not just the model provider or the app developer.
For buildersTreat AI routers and proxies as data processors: audit what they log or retain, avoid sending secrets through them, and use canary-style testing to verify behavior.

Pros

  • The canary methodology gives measurable evidence rather than speculation
  • The findings can drive better standards for routing services

Cons

  • Many apps are not aware they depend on these intermediaries, so exposure may be widespread
  • Fixing the supply chain requires coordination across many vendors

Thinking Machines releases open-weight Inkling 2 Mini, a looped transformer for local use

First reported 2026-11-20 · Latest update 2026-11-21

Thinking Machines Lab released Inkling 2 Mini, an open-weight model with 26.4B total parameters and 3.4B active, under Apache 2.0. Instead of adding weights, it reuses its middle layers three times per token, keeping the checkpoint near 20GB so it can run on a 32GB Mac or a single DGX Spark. The release has up to 1M-token context and is positioned for agentic coding, tool use, and document work, with hosted inference and fine-tuning on Tinker. The capability claims are the lab's; the candidates include no independent benchmarks.

Why it mattersA capable, permissively licensed model that runs on consumer hardware lowers the cost of private, local agentic workflows.
For buildersIt is a candidate for local coding-agent or document pipelines where data must stay on-device, and it is worth benchmarking against your own tasks.

Pros

  • Apache 2.0 license and local deployment options reduce vendor lock-in and data exposure
  • Looped layer reuse is a different efficiency approach from simply shrinking models

Cons

  • Capability claims are company-reported and not independently verified in the candidates
  • Looping adds per-token compute, so real-world speed on consumer hardware needs testing

One Thing to Explore

Inkling 2 Mini and looped transformers (reusing middle layers per token)

Instead of scaling parameters, this model reuses its middle layers several times per token, trading extra per-token compute for a smaller checkpoint that fits on a laptop. Working through how looping changes memory, latency, and quality tradeoffs compared with standard dense or MoE models is a good way to build intuition about inference efficiency design.
